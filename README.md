# Vic3-Corporatism-Rework
This mod can be considered to be in the early stages of development.

Ever found Fascism and Corporatism to be somewhat dull in the base game? Fascists being essentially a reskin of Ethnonationalists and Traditionalists? Well not anymore!
With this mod I wanted to add some flavour and more depth to both Fascism and Corporatism by introducing new variants of Fascism (Classical, Clerical and National Socialist), alongside repeatable 4 Year Plans where you can choose a goal to achieve beforehand for bonuses upon completion (or malices when failing!). Venture out to reorganise society into one state-managed organism with new amendments granted by completing Journal Entries.

Bug reports are welcome in the comments alongside suggestions. I plan to continue the development of this little mod in my free time.

Compatibility:
Almost sure to not be compatible with mods like BPM and Laws+ as of this moment. Eventually will work on a compatibilty patch unless someone beats me to it for some reason.
